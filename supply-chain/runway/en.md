# Supply Chain Runway

1. **SBOM-first** — generate CycloneDX SBOM for every artifact.
2. **Continuous monitoring** — feed SBOMs into Dependency-Track.
3. **Fail fast** — block builds with critical-severity dependencies.
4. **Provenance** — sign releases with cosign; verify in production.
5. **Inventory** — maintain a software bill of materials for all production services.

Reference: [OWASP SCVS](https://github.com/OWASP/Software-Component-Verification-Standard)
