# Threat Modeling Skills

- **STRIDE/LINDDUN** — apply structured threat categorization.
- **Data Flow Diagramming** — model architecture in pytm or Threat Dragon.
- **Attack Tree Construction** — decompose high-risk scenarios into actionable paths.
- **Risk Rating** — use DREAD or CVSS to prioritize threats.
- **Threat Model as Code** — version-controlled threat models in CI/CD.
